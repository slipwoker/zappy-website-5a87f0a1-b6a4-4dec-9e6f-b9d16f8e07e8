Our Firm's Values

The principles that guide every investigation and every professional decision we make

01

Absolute Discretion

All information that reaches us is kept under full and unwavering confidentiality. We exercise the utmost discretion at every stage — from the first meeting to the delivery of the final report — and ensure that the identity of our clients, the circumstances of the inquiry, and the contents of the investigation never reach unauthorized hands.

02

Uncompromising Professionalism

Whether it involves locating hidden assets, exposing internal embezzlement, or conducting discreet personal inquiries — our team combines expertise from the intelligence community with advanced field methodologies, to deliver clear and verified evidence for any personal, legal, or corporate challenge.

03

Transparency with the Client

We believe that an informed client is a secure client. That is why we communicate clearly about timelines, scope of work, interim findings, and the limits of what is possible. We do not promise what we cannot deliver, and we always provide a complete and reliable picture — even when the answer is not what the client expected to hear.

04

Strategic Thinking

Every investigation is managed as an intelligence mission: preliminary mapping, defining objectives, selecting a precise methodology, and analyzing findings that enable the client to make informed decisions. Our findings are a strategic working tool — not just a report.

05

Humanity & Sensitivity

Behind every case stands a person — sometimes under pressure, uncertainty, or business harm. We treat every client with respect, attentiveness, and genuine empathy. Our human approach does not conflict with professional sharpness — it is an inseparable part of it, and enables optimal collaboration throughout the entire process.

About the Firm

Intelligence Expertise. Field Experience. Proven Results.

Professional Methodology

Our Work Process

Every investigation is conducted according to a structured, transparent, and lawful methodology — from the first meeting to the final findings. This is how we ensure absolute discretion, professionalism, and strategic support at every stage.

01

Confidential Initial Assessment

A structured and discreet introductory meeting in which we listen to the need, examine the scope of the request, and verify its legal feasibility. All information provided is kept in absolute confidentiality.

02

Scope Definition & Strategy

Together we define the investigation's objectives, timeline, required resources, and success criteria. At this stage, a written and binding work plan is agreed upon — due diligence for companies in Israel and abroad is included as needed.

03

Investigation & Information Gathering

Where necessary, the team of analysts, veterans of the defense establishment, carries out the financial investigation, business intelligence, digital surveillance, and evidence gathering — while strictly adhering to the law and the principles of professional ethics.

04

Findings & Strategic Guidance

We deliver a structured, clear, and actionable findings report — suitable for legal, business, or managerial use. Personal guidance by Yair Bloom ensures that the conclusions are translated into informed decisions.
